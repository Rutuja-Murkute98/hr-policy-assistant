# Benefits by Band Summary

**Version:** 1.0 | **Effective Date:** 1 April 2026 | **Policy Owner:** HR

This summary brings together the entitlements that depend on an employee's band. It is a test document for reading
uploaded files: every figure is taken from the full policies, which take precedence.

## 1. Hospital Room Rent

The group mediclaim policy covers hospital room rent according to the employee's band.

| Band | Room category | Room rent limit per day |
|---|---|---|
| Band 1 to Band 3 | Shared or general ward | Rs. 3,000 |
| Band 4 to Band 5 | Single standard room | Rs. 5,000 |
| Band 6 and above | Single deluxe room | Rs. 8,000 |

## 2. Learning and Development Budget

Each employee may use the following yearly amount for sponsored external training and certification.

| Band | Yearly budget |
|---|---|
| Band 1 to Band 3 | Rs. 20,000 |
| Band 4 to Band 5 | Rs. 40,000 |
| Band 6 and above | Rs. 75,000 |

## 3. Salary Advance Limits

Confirmed employees with at least 12 months of service since confirmation may take an interest-free salary advance.

| Band | Maximum advance |
|---|---|
| Band 1 to Band 3 | 1 month's gross salary |
| Band 4 to Band 5 | 1.5 months' gross salary |
| Band 6 and above | 2 months' gross salary |

In all cases, the advance cannot exceed Rs. 2,50,000.

## 4. Referral Bonus

The referral bonus depends on the band of the role being filled, not the band of the employee who refers.

| Band of the role | Referral bonus |
|---|---|
| Band 1 to Band 2 | Rs. 15,000 |
| Band 3 to Band 4 | Rs. 30,000 |
| Band 5 to Band 6 | Rs. 50,000 |
| Band 7 and above | Rs. 75,000 |

## 5. Probation Period

| Band | Probation period |
|---|---|
| Band 1 to Band 3 | 3 months |
| Band 4 to Band 6 | 6 months |
| Band 7 and above | 6 months |

## 6. Leave Entitlements

| Leave type | Entitlement | Who it applies to |
|---|---|---|
| Earned Leave | 18 days a year | Confirmed employees |
| Sick Leave | 12 days a year | All employees, including those on probation |
| Casual Leave | 6 days a year | All employees |
| Paternity Leave | 15 days | Within 3 months of birth or adoption, for up to 2 children |
| Maternity Leave | 26 weeks | For the first two children, after 80 days of work in the last 12 months |
