**At a Glance:** Rutuja Talent Nexus Pvt. Ltd. is an HR-technology and talent solutions company founded in 2018 and headquartered in Nashik, Maharashtra, with branch offices in Pune and Mumbai. The company employs around 450 people and is led by Founder and CEO Ms. Rutuja Patil.

## 1. Who We Are

Rutuja Talent Nexus Pvt. Ltd. is an HR-technology and talent solutions company founded in 2018 in Nashik, Maharashtra. The company was started by Ms. Rutuja Patil with a team of 6 people working out of a small office on College Road, with the vision of making high-quality recruitment technology accessible to companies beyond India's largest metros. Today, Rutuja Talent Nexus employs approximately 450 people across its three offices and serves over 300 corporate clients across India.

## 2. What We Do

Rutuja Talent Nexus operates three complementary lines of business. NexusHire is the company's flagship SaaS product: an AI-assisted applicant tracking and recruitment platform used by mid-market companies to manage job postings, candidate pipelines, assessments, and offer workflows. Talent Services is the company's recruitment process outsourcing (RPO) and permanent staffing practice, through which dedicated delivery teams run end-to-end hiring for client organisations. HR Advisory provides consulting on compensation benchmarking, HR policy design, and talent analytics to growing companies.

## 5. Company Facts

Rutuja Talent Nexus was incorporated in 2018 as a private limited company under the Companies Act 2013. The company is headquartered in Nashik, Maharashtra, and has approximately 450 employees as of the current financial year. The company's financial year runs from 1 April to 31 March, and the leave calendar runs from 1 January to 31 December. The employee HRMS portal is hrms.rutujatalentnexus.in, the careers site is careers.rutujatalentnexus.in, and the general HR helpdesk is hr@rutujatalentnexus.in.

## 6. Mission and Values

The mission of Rutuja Talent Nexus is to connect great companies with great people through technology, insight, and integrity. The company's values are: People First, meaning decisions consider the impact on employees, candidates, and clients before anything else; Ownership, meaning every employee is trusted and expected to own outcomes end to end; Craft, meaning the company takes pride in the quality of its products and services; and Integrity, meaning the company does the right thing even when it is difficult or expensive.
