# Employee FAQ

**Company:** Rutuja Talent Nexus Pvt. Ltd. | **Registered Office:** 3rd Floor, Nexus House, College Road, Nashik - 422005, Maharashtra, India
**Version:** 2.0 | **Effective Date:** 1 April 2026 | **Next Review:** 1 April 2027 | **Policy Owner:** Human Resources Department

**At a Glance:** Quick answers to the most common employee questions about leave, payroll, work from home, insurance, exits, and IT at Rutuja Talent Nexus. Each answer names the governing policy; if this FAQ ever differs from a policy document, the policy document prevails.

## 1. Leave and Holidays

How many leaves do I get in a year? Confirmed employees get 18 days of Earned Leave, 12 days of Sick Leave, and 6 days of Casual Leave per calendar year, plus 12 public holidays. See the Leave Policy for details.

Can I carry forward unused leave? Only Earned Leave carries forward, up to a maximum of 10 days; anything above that lapses at year end. Sick Leave and Casual Leave lapse every December.

How do I apply for leave? Through the HRMS portal at hrms.rutujatalentnexus.in. Planned leave needs 7 working days' advance application; for emergencies, notify your manager before 9:30 AM and file the request the same day.

What is the maternity and paternity leave entitlement? Maternity leave is 26 weeks of paid leave for the first two children (12 weeks for the third onward). Paternity leave is 15 days, to be taken within 3 months of birth or adoption.

Which festival holidays do we get? The 12 public holidays include national holidays plus Maharashtra festival holidays such as Gudi Padwa, Maharashtra Day (1 May), Ganesh Chaturthi, and Diwali. The exact list is published on the intranet each January.

## 2. Salary and Payroll

When is salary credited? On or before the last working day of each month, to the bank account registered in HRMS.

When are payslips available? On the HRMS Payroll section by the 5th of the following month. The payslip password is your date of birth in DDMMYYYY format.

What is deducted from my salary? Employee Provident Fund (12 percent of basic salary), Professional Tax (Rs. 200 per month, Rs. 300 in February, per the Maharashtra slab), and TDS based on your chosen tax regime and declarations.

I found an error in my salary. What do I do? Raise a Payroll Query in HRMS within 30 days of the payslip. The payroll team responds within 5 working days, and confirmed corrections are paid with the next cycle.

Can I take a salary advance? Confirmed employees with 12 or more months of service can request an interest-free advance (up to 1 month's gross salary for Bands 1-3). An emergency advance of up to Rs. 25,000 is available regardless of band. See the Salary Advance Policy.

## 3. Work From Home and Attendance

How many days can I work from home? Rutuja Talent Nexus follows a hybrid model: minimum 3 days in office per week, up to 2 WFH days. You must be confirmed with a performance rating of 3 or above to be eligible.

What are the working hours? 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM with a 15-minute grace period, and core hours of 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM on WFH days. Flexi-time windows are available with manager approval.

Can I work remotely for a longer stretch? Yes, Extended Remote Work of up to 30 working days per calendar year can be requested for relocation, medical, or family-care reasons. See the Work From Home Policy.

## 4. Insurance and Benefits

What health insurance do I have? A group mediclaim policy with a base sum insured of Rs. 5,00,000 per policy year, covering you, your spouse, and children up to age 25 at no cost. Parents can be added at a 50 percent subsidised premium.

How do I use cashless hospitalisation? Present your insurance e-card (downloadable from the HRMS Benefits section) at any of the 8,000+ network hospitals. For planned admissions, request pre-authorisation through the insurer helpline listed on your e-card.

What other benefits do I get? Group term life insurance at 3 times annual CTC, EPF, gratuity after 5 years of service, corporate NPS with a company contribution for eligible opted-in employees, and a Flexible Benefits Plan for Band 4 and above. See the Compensation and Benefits Policy.

## 5. Performance and Growth

When does the appraisal happen? The cycle runs April to March; reviews are conducted in March and increments and promotions take effect from 1 April. A mid-year check-in happens in October.

When am I eligible for promotion? After at least 18 months in your current band with a Rating 4 or 5 in the current cycle. The minimum salary increase on promotion is 15 percent of CTC.

What training can the company sponsor? Professional certifications (such as AWS, Azure, and GCP), external courses, and part-time higher education, within band-wise annual budgets. Apply through the HRMS Learning Module. See the Training and Learning Development Policy.

## 6. Joining, Probation, and Exit

How long is probation? 6 months as standard, or 3 months for Bands 1-3, extendable once by up to 3 months. Confirmation requires a clean background verification.

What is my notice period? 2 weeks while on probation and 3 months once confirmed. Notice period buyout is possible with management and HR approval.

When do I get my final settlement and relieving letter? Full and Final settlement is processed within 45 days of your last working day, and the relieving and experience letters are issued within 7 working days after FnF.

Can I refer a friend for a job? Yes. Submit the referral through the HRMS Referral Module before the candidate applies. The referral bonus is paid 50 percent when the hire completes 3 months and 50 percent at 6 months.

## 7. IT, Security, and Conduct

My laptop is lost or stolen. What do I do? File an FIR with the local police immediately and share a copy with IT and HR within 48 hours. IT will remotely wipe the device. For damage, report to the IT helpdesk within 24 hours.

Can I use ChatGPT or other AI tools for work? Only tools on the company's Approved AI Tools List, and never with confidential, client, or personal data. Review everything an AI tool produces before using it. See the AI Tool Usage Policy.

Can I do freelance work on the side? Not without prior written approval. Moonlighting and competing engagements are prohibited; unpaid volunteering and academic guest lectures with a small honorarium are exempt. See the Moonlighting and Conflict of Interest Policy.

How do I report harassment or an ethics concern? For sexual harassment, write to the Internal Complaints Committee at icc@rutujatalentnexus.in within 3 months of the incident. For fraud or ethics concerns, use ethics@rutujatalentnexus.in (anonymous reports accepted). For other grievances, use the HRMS Grievance Module or grievance@rutujatalentnexus.in.
