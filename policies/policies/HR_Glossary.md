# HR Terms and Glossary

**At a Glance:** Definitions of HR terms and abbreviations (CTC, EL, PIP, FnF, ATS, EPF, and more) as used across all Rutuja Talent Nexus policies. Where a number stated here differs from a policy document, the governing policy prevails.

## 1. Policy Brief and Purpose

This glossary defines key Human Resources terms, concepts, and abbreviations used across all HR policies, communications, and processes at Rutuja Talent Nexus Pvt. Ltd. Employees and managers are encouraged to refer to this document when they encounter unfamiliar HR terminology in policies, emails, or appraisal documents. This glossary is maintained and updated annually by the HR team.

## 2. Compensation and Payroll Terms

CTC (Cost to Company) refers to the total compensation an employer spends on an employee, including basic salary, allowances, bonuses, employer PF contribution, gratuity provisioning, and all other perquisites. At Rutuja Talent Nexus, CTC is the standard measure used in offer letters and annual increment communications.

Basic Salary is the fixed core component of an employee's monthly pay, constituting 40 percent of CTC at Rutuja Talent Nexus. It forms the base for calculating PF, gratuity, and leave encashment.

HRA (House Rent Allowance) is a salary component paid to employees to cover rental housing expenses. At Rutuja Talent Nexus, HRA is 20 percent of CTC and is eligible for tax exemption under Section 10(13A) of the Income Tax Act subject to conditions.

Variable Pay is the performance-linked component of compensation paid annually based on individual ratings and company financial performance. At Rutuja Talent Nexus, variable pay constitutes 15 percent of CTC.

Gross Salary is the total monthly salary before deductions including PF, professional tax, and TDS.

Net Salary or Take-Home Salary is the amount credited to the employee's account after all statutory and voluntary deductions.

Retroactive Pay is compensation paid to an employee for work performed in a previous period, typically resulting from a delayed salary revision. Rutuja Talent Nexus processes retroactive pay adjustments within 30 days of the effective revision date.

Payroll Deduction is the amount withheld from an employee's salary for statutory purposes such as EPF contribution, professional tax, and income tax deducted at source.

Overtime refers to work performed beyond standard working hours. At Rutuja Talent Nexus, software employees are not entitled to overtime pay but are eligible for Compensatory Off as per the Attendance Policy.

Wage is the hourly monetary compensation for work performed, applicable to contractual or hourly workers. Full-time employees at Rutuja Talent Nexus receive fixed monthly salaries.

## 3. Leave and Attendance Terms

Earned Leave (EL) also called Annual Leave is paid time off accrued based on service. At Rutuja Talent Nexus, all confirmed employees receive 18 EL days per calendar year.

Sick Leave (SL) is paid leave provided for personal illness or medical appointments. Rutuja Talent Nexus provides 12 sick leave days per year.

Casual Leave (CL) is leave for urgent personal matters or emergencies. Rutuja Talent Nexus provides 6 casual leave days per year.

Loss of Pay (LOP) refers to the deduction of salary for days an employee is absent beyond their approved leave balance.

Floating Holidays are flexible paid time off days that employees can use at their discretion for personal observances or preferences, separate from the standard public holiday list.

Garden Leave is a period during which an employee who has resigned is required to stay away from the workplace while still being paid. It is used to protect business interests during the notice period. Rutuja Talent Nexus may invoke garden leave for senior employees in sensitive roles.

Absenteeism refers to the frequent or prolonged absence of employees without valid reasons. Chronic absenteeism at Rutuja Talent Nexus is addressed under the Attendance and Disciplinary Policy.

Compensatory Off (Comp Off) is a day off granted to an employee in lieu of working on a weekend or public holiday as directed by a manager. At Rutuja Talent Nexus, comp off must be availed within 30 days of accrual.

## 4. Performance Management Terms

Performance Management Cycle is the continuous process of setting goals, monitoring progress, providing feedback, and evaluating performance. At Rutuja Talent Nexus, the formal cycle runs annually from April to March.

KPI (Key Performance Indicator) is a quantifiable metric used to evaluate individual, team, or organisational performance against defined targets.

OKR (Objectives and Key Results) is a goal-setting framework defining specific objectives and measurable outcomes. Rutuja Talent Nexus uses SMART goals within the annual appraisal cycle.

SMART Goals are goals that are Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound. All Rutuja Talent Nexus employees set SMART goals at the beginning of each appraisal cycle in April.

360-Degree Feedback is a feedback process collecting input from multiple sources including managers, peers, direct reports, and self-assessment. Rutuja Talent Nexus incorporates 360-degree elements in senior-level appraisals.

PIP (Performance Improvement Plan) is a formal, time-bound plan created for employees whose performance is below expectations, defining improvement targets, timelines, and support mechanisms. At Rutuja Talent Nexus, PIPs run for 60 to 90 days.

eNPS (Employee Net Promoter Score) is a metric measuring employee satisfaction by gauging how likely employees are to recommend the company as a great place to work.

Pulse Surveys are short, frequent surveys to gather real-time employee feedback. Rutuja Talent Nexus conducts quarterly pulse surveys through the HRMS portal.

## 5. Recruitment and Onboarding Terms

ATS (Applicant Tracking System) is software that manages job postings, applications, and candidate tracking. Rutuja Talent Nexus uses an ATS integrated with its career portal at careers.rutujatalentnexus.in.

Talent Acquisition is the strategic process of attracting, identifying, and hiring skilled individuals to meet business needs.

Employer Branding is the process of promoting Rutuja Talent Nexus as a desirable employer to attract and retain top talent.

Preboarding is the process of engaging new hires between acceptance of the offer and the official start date. Rutuja Talent Nexus sends welcome kits, access credentials, and orientation schedules during preboarding.

Employee Onboarding Software refers to the technology used to manage the onboarding experience. At Rutuja Talent Nexus, onboarding is managed through the HRMS portal at hrms.rutujatalentnexus.in.

Background Verification is the process of verifying candidate information including employment history, educational qualifications, identity, and criminal records. All offers at Rutuja Talent Nexus are subject to successful background verification.

Cost Per Hire is the total cost incurred to hire a new employee, including job posting fees, recruiter costs, and interview expenses.

Recruitment Marketing is the strategic use of marketing to attract potential candidates to Rutuja Talent Nexus as an employer of choice.

## 6. Employment and Legal Terms

EEO (Equal Employment Opportunity) is the principle ensuring all individuals have equal chances of employment regardless of gender, caste, religion, age, or disability. Rutuja Talent Nexus is an equal opportunity employer.

Discrimination refers to unfair treatment of individuals based on protected characteristics such as gender, caste, religion, age, or disability. Discrimination is strictly prohibited at Rutuja Talent Nexus under the Code of Conduct Policy.

Hostile Work Environment is a workplace where harassment, discrimination, or intimidating behaviour makes employees uncomfortable. Rutuja Talent Nexus addresses hostile work environment complaints under the POSH Policy and Grievance Redressal Policy.

Workplace Bullying refers to repeated inappropriate behaviour that creates a hostile or humiliating work environment. It is addressed under the Disciplinary Policy at Rutuja Talent Nexus.

Confidentiality refers to the protection of sensitive employee and company information. All Rutuja Talent Nexus employees sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement at joining.

Disciplinary Action refers to corrective measures taken by the company in response to policy violations or misconduct, ranging from verbal warnings to termination.

Grievance is a formal complaint raised by an employee regarding workplace issues. Rutuja Talent Nexus has a structured 4-level Grievance Redressal Policy.

## 7. HR Operations Terms

HRIS (Human Resources Information System) is an integrated software centralising HR data and processes. At Rutuja Talent Nexus, the HRIS is accessible at hrms.rutujatalentnexus.in.

HR Software refers to technology solutions automating HR processes including payroll, leave, performance, and recruitment.

Employee Lifecycle refers to the stages an employee goes through from recruitment, onboarding, development, performance management, and exit.

Employee Engagement refers to the emotional and psychological commitment employees have towards their work and organisation.

People Operations is a strategic approach to HR that focuses on building positive employee experiences. Rutuja Talent Nexus's HR team functions as a People Operations team.

Succession Planning is the process of identifying and developing internal employees for future leadership roles. Rutuja Talent Nexus conducts annual succession planning for Band 5 and above roles.

Employer Value Proposition (EVP) is the unique set of benefits Rutuja Talent Nexus offers to attract and retain talent, including competitive pay, hybrid work, career growth, and health benefits.

Employee Referral Program is Rutuja Talent Nexus' structured programme rewarding employees for referring candidates who are successfully hired and complete their first 6 months of employment.

## 8. Separation and Exit Terms

Attrition is the natural reduction in workforce size through voluntary resignations, retirements, or other forms of separation.

Turnover Rate is the percentage of employees who leave the company in a given period, used to assess workforce stability.

Exit Interview is a structured conversation between departing employees and HR to gather feedback and understand reasons for resignation. Rutuja Talent Nexus conducts exit interviews for all voluntarily separating employees.

Full and Final Settlement (FnF) is the final payroll computation made after an employee's last working day, including outstanding salary, leave encashment, gratuity if applicable, and other dues or deductions.

Gratuity is a statutory benefit paid to employees upon separation after completing a minimum of 5 years of continuous service, calculated under the Payment of Gratuity Act 1972.

Garden Leave is described under the Leave terms above and applies in the separation context when an employee is asked to work out the notice period away from the office.

Bumping is a process in which an employee facing a layoff may displace another employee in a lower-level role if they have seniority and meet the role requirements.

Layoff is the temporary or permanent termination of employment for a group of employees due to business reasons such as restructuring or economic conditions.

Downshifting refers to an employee voluntarily moving to a lower-responsibility role or reduced working hours.

## 9. Modern HR and Workplace Culture Terms

Quiet Quitting refers to employees who remain employed but disengage and limit their effort to only the minimum required by their job description, without formally resigning.

Quiet Firing refers to a management approach of gradually making an employee's work conditions uncomfortable to encourage voluntary resignation rather than formally terminating them. This practice is prohibited at Rutuja Talent Nexus and may be raised as a grievance.

The Great Resignation refers to the large-scale trend of employees voluntarily resigning from jobs, particularly prominent globally post-2021, driven by changing work preferences and priorities.

Moonlighting refers to an employee holding a second job or engaging in paid work outside their primary employment. Rutuja Talent Nexus' position on moonlighting is detailed in the Moonlighting and Conflict of Interest Policy.

Hustle Culture refers to a workplace culture celebrating excessive hard work and long hours. Rutuja Talent Nexus does not promote hustle culture and encourages sustainable productivity and work-life balance.

Employee Burnout is a state of chronic physical and emotional exhaustion caused by prolonged job stress. Rutuja Talent Nexus addresses employee burnout through the Employee Assistance Programme and flexible work options.

Telecommuting or Remote Work refers to working from a location outside the company office using technology. Rutuja Talent Nexus' remote work guidelines are defined in the Work From Home Policy.

Digital Nomad refers to a remote worker who works while travelling. Rutuja Talent Nexus' WFH policy does not currently support digital nomad arrangements for more than 30 days without HR Director approval.

Skills Gap refers to the difference between skills required by the organisation and those possessed by employees. Rutuja Talent Nexus addresses skills gaps through the Learning and Development programme.

Labor Hoarding is the practice of retaining more employees than necessary to avoid future rehiring challenges, typically observed during economic downturns.

## 10. Benefits and Statutory Terms

EPF (Employees Provident Fund) is a statutory retirement savings scheme under the EPF Act 1952, to which both employees and Rutuja Talent Nexus contribute 12 percent of basic salary each.

ESOP (Employee Stock Ownership Plan) is a scheme through which Rutuja Talent Nexus grants eligible employees the right to purchase company shares at a predetermined price after a vesting period. Detailed terms are described in the ESOP Policy.

Gratuity is covered under the Separation Terms above and the Resignation and Exit Policy.

NPS (National Pension System) is a voluntary retirement savings scheme. Rutuja Talent Nexus contributes an additional 10 percent of basic salary for employees who opt into corporate NPS after 2 years of service.

Professional Tax is a state-level tax deducted from the employee's monthly salary as per the applicable state tax slab. For Rutuja Talent Nexus employees based in Maharashtra, the slab is Rs. 200 per month (Rs. 300 in February), totalling Rs. 2,500 per annum.

TDS (Tax Deducted at Source) is income tax deducted monthly from employee salaries by Rutuja Talent Nexus on behalf of the Income Tax Department as per the employee's declared tax regime.
